Every Move Changes the Board.

Take control of the Gray Square. Slide across the grid, swap colors, and weave complex patterns out of chaos in this elegant puzzle experience.

One Simple Rule, Infinite Possibilities.

Your goal is simple: recreate the target pattern. But your only tool is the Gray Square itself. As you guide it across the grid, it instantly swaps its currently held color with the tile beneath it.

You'll need to think steps ahead. Where do you drop the red tile? How do you retrieve the blue one? Every movement leaves a trail that reshapes the puzzle.

From Simple Lines to Brilliant Mosaics.

What starts with basic shapes quickly escalates into complex, multicolored challenges. You'll progress from solving single-color crosses to carefully unravelling brilliant rainbow patterns.

With an aesthetic, minimalistic design and a soothing atmosphere, The Gray Square offers the perfect environment to relax while pushing your spatial logic to its limits.
