Simple Mechanic, Deep Strategy.

The goal is simple: match the target pattern on the grid. To do this, you can pick up a colored tile and swap it with any adjacent tile. Every swap brings you one step closer to the solution or deeper into the puzzle. With a limited number of moves, every decision counts.

Evolving Challenges.

What begins with simple lines and squares quickly evolves into complex, multi-colored mosaics. The Gray Square is designed to grow with you, continuously introducing new patterns and color combinations that demand new ways of thinking. Each level pack is a new chapter in your puzzle-solving journey.
