# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 3, 2026

Thank you for playing **The Gray Square**. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application and website.

## 1. Information We Collect
**We do not collect any personally identifiable information.** The Gray Square is designed to be a standalone logic puzzle game that respects your privacy. We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personal data to play.

## 2. Automatically Collected Data & Analytics
To help us improve the game, we may collect anonymous, aggregated data regarding app performance, crashes, and general gameplay metrics. This data is completely unidentifiable and helps us understand which puzzles are too hard, or if any bugs need fixing. This is strictly meant to improve the overall player experience.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The game is built using the Unity engine, which may collect standard device hardware information for crash reporting and optimization purposes in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## 4. Children's Privacy
The Gray Square does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. Given that we do not collect personal data, children are safe to use and enjoy the application.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this page periodically.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at [graysquare99@gmail.com](mailto:graysquare99@gmail.com).
